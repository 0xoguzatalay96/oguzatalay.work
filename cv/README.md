# Oğuz Atalay CV Pack

This folder contains role-targeted CV drafts and positioning notes for applications through oguzatalay.work.

## Canonical Source

Use `Oguz_Atalay_CV_Master_Source_Bank.md` as the main CV source bank. It is not the CV to send directly; it is the master content library used to generate role-specific CVs.

## Files

- `Oguz_Atalay_Influencer_Marketing_Digital_Assets_CV.md`
  - Direct application CV for Marketing Manager, Integrated Marketing, Growth, Brand, Partnerships, and Campaign Operations roles. This is the clean version to send for marketing-first applications.
- `Oguz_Atalay_Master_CV.md`
  - Long full-profile CV draft. Keep as reference, but do not treat it as the canonical source.
- `Oguz_Atalay_CV_Master_Source_Bank.md`
  - Full CV content bank: title options, summary variants, role-specific positioning, metrics, master bullets, project references, tools, ATS keywords, application assembly notes, and cover letter starter.
- `Oguz_Atalay_Crypto_Web3_Community_Growth_CV.md`
  - For crypto exchanges, digital asset teams, banks entering crypto, fintechs, Web3 gaming, NFT/metaverse ecosystems, and community/KOL/growth roles.
- `Oguz_Atalay_Crypto_Web3_Community_Growth_CV_TR.md`
  - Turkish version for crypto exchanges, digital asset teams, banks entering crypto, fintechs, and Web3 community/KOL/growth roles.
- `Oguz_Atalay_Brand_Agency_Creator_Marketing_CV.md`
  - For agencies, brands, retail, fashion, gaming, consumer tech, PR, creator partnerships, and influencer marketing roles.
- `Oguz_Atalay_Marka_Ajans_Kreator_Marketing_CV_TR.md`
  - Turkish version for agencies, brands, retail, fashion, gaming, consumer tech, PR, creator partnerships, and influencer marketing roles.

## Positioning

The strongest shared narrative is not only "influencer marketing." It is:

> Creator-led growth operator with 10+ years of campaign leadership, Web3 community operations, creator/KOL coordination, market intelligence, and executive-ready reporting.

Use the crypto CV when the role asks for community, KOL, exchange growth, token/NFT launches, social sentiment, community intelligence, or digital assets strategy.

Use the brand/agency CV when the role asks for creator partnerships, influencer campaigns, PR activations, retail/fashion/gaming campaigns, campaign reporting, or brand-safe production.
